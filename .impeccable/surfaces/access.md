# Signup, login and manual UPI payment

Extends Gummy Bear's existing cream/pastel world. Preserve the photo-to-ideas page and in-memory photo while showing access screens. Use a centered white rounded panel, purple actions, Nunito and clear form labels. On mobile, keep controls and QR within the viewport.

Signup and login use labeled email/password inputs with loading and retry states. Payment shows ₹99 a month, the owner's supplied QR, manual-confirmation wording and the account's one-time free button when eligible. A missing QR is explicitly reported rather than replaced with invented payment data. Paid confirmation returns to the existing tool.
