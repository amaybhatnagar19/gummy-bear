# Milestone 1 play page

Mode: operate. A parent supplies one toy photo and an age, then receives toy names and three concrete ideas.

Inherits DESIGN.md: warm cream, pastel lavender/peach/mint, rounded white photo panel, Nunito, dark charcoal text, purple actions, generous spacing. The purple action uses a darker shade for readable white text. No change to the broader design brief.

Desktop: photo and age controls beside the result area. Mobile: controls then results. Empty, photo preparation, AI loading, recoverable error, no toys and successful reply states all stay on this page. Result appears with a 250ms fade and six-pixel rise; reduced-motion settings disable it.

No generated illustration, landing-page sections, account prompt or history. Simple geometric empty-state shapes keep the task visible.
