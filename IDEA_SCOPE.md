IDEA LOCK · Build Sprint

The idea, in one line: Building an AI-native assistant that helps parents of kids between 2 to 8 years find, personalize, and extend the joy of toys—by blending discovery, emotional intelligence, and playful delight.

Why me (at least 1 of 3: an audience that trusts me, years inside the workflow, data nobody else has): I am a father of almost 4 years old twin kids and have a friend circle in which they all are parents of the kids ranging from the age of 2 to 8 years of age.

GOAL

Delta 4 (the steps today → the steps with my product): 

Steps Today → Either the toys are gifted to the kids by the relatives or parents purchase the toys based on the generic options available in the market which is generally bifurcated as age or Gender bracket like a Car for 2-5 years old boy or a Doll for for 3-4 years of Girl. And then there are some toys which focuses on the overall development of child and are grouped age wise but then they are also generic and is one for all option. There are no personalised options available. On the other hand the parent are in a fomo or a delima that are they being efficient and understanding parent to their child or not. How can the parents behaviour and habits affect/shape their child’s growth and the overall personality they currently do google research or go to chat gpt with generic answers.

Steps in my Product → 

1. Discover Mode → Answer 5 fun questions and get personalized toy recommendations that match your child's interests, age, and your budget!
2. Companion Mode → Get creative ideas, game hacks, and mini-missions to extend playtime and make every toy more engaging!
3. Reuse Reminder → Gentle nudges to rediscover forgotten toys with fresh ideas or suggestions for donating to spread joy!
4. Playful Chat → Chat with this tool anytime! Get parenting tips, celebration ideas, Situation based solutions to the queries and answers to all your toy questions!

The core loop (user stories, written by me):

| Mode | Function |
| --- | --- |
| **Discovery Mode** | Asks parents 3–5 playful, intelligent questions and recommends curated toy picks personalized to their child |
| **Companion Mode** | Provides post-purchase ideas, game hacks, and mini-missions to extend toy engagement |
| **Reuse Reminder Mode** | AI based model helps to Gently nudge the parents to reuse/donate/discover value from toys that are gathering dust |
| **Playful Chat** | Parents chat with this tool, Based on the LLMs and the available data it gives parenting tips, celebration ideas, Situation based solutions to the queries and answers to all the toy relatedquestions! |

| Feature | Description |
| --- | --- |
| ✨ GPT-powered Conversational Flow | Delightful, playful tone; asks context-rich questions like “What makes your child giggle for hours?” |
| 🧸 Toy Recommender Engine | Maps toy catalog (CSV or JSON) to age, interest, learning goal, price, durabilit |
| 🔄 Engagement Generator | For each toy, suggests creative prompts like “3 ways to turn this into a family game night” |
| 📦 Reuse Nudger (optional) | “Haven’t played with this toy in 30 days? Want ideas or donate it to a local school?” |
